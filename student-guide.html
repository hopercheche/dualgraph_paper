RESEARCH AND STUDY MATERIALS

Participant instructions

Natural consultation, followed by brief experience feedback.

Before taking part

This study examines how an AI doctor or patient responds during a pharmacy consultation and how the system evaluates the consultation. Participation is voluntary and does not affect course grades. The research assistant will explain recording, data retention and withdrawal before asking for consent.

Use only the anonymous participant code provided by the research team. Do not enter names, student numbers, contact details or real patient information.

What to do

  1. Practise the microphone, text entry, start and finish controls using a non-study example.
  2. Read the assigned station brief and use only the resources permitted in that brief.
  3. Conduct the consultation as you normally would: ask questions, explain your recommendation and correct yourself when needed. You do not need to memorise a script.
  4. If there is a technical problem, alert the research assistant. The assistant can help with equipment but will not supply clinical answers.
  5. Finish when your assigned task or time limit is reached. If another station is assigned, follow the assistant’s instructions before opening feedback.
  6. After viewing the available feedback, complete the short experience questionnaire. Select “Unable to judge” for feedback you have not seen.

Students do not write benchmark scripts, label conversations or run ablations. System assignment is managed by the research team and is not displayed in the consultation interface.
