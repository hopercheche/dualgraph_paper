R05 · Virtual patients and OSCEs

PWP is a direct predecessor for controlled disclosure

Patients With Personality: Realistic Patient Simulation through Controlled Diversity and Selective Disclosure

Moritz Schlager et al. · 2026
arXiv:2606.17441; preprint

Publisher / author sourceOriginal local PDF (22 pages)Cited passages and highlightsBack to references

What the study investigates

Generic virtual patients can be uniformly cooperative, articulate, and willing to disclose information. This limits personality variation and the need for follow-up questioning in clinical practice.

How it was studied

Patients With Personality separates personality conditions from case facts. A disclosure grid provides alternative expressions with different degrees of clarity. At each turn, the system identifies the queried fields and lets the conversational model access the currently available content. Evaluation uses 22 case interactions from ACI-Bench and ratings of selected transcripts by seven residents.

Findings

PWP outperforms some compared simulators on realism and reduced over-disclosure, and personality traits can be recognized to some extent. The results support controlled disclosure, although not all personality dimensions are equally recognizable.

Limits of the evidence

Locking facts and releasing them after appropriate questions already has a precedent. The disclosure grid primarily controls patient generation; its results do not establish correct attribution of pharmacy-student evidence or binding to a particular medication regimen.

How it informs DualGraph-OSCE

DualGraph-OSCE retains controlled disclosure but records when a fact becomes authorized, when it is actually delivered, whether the learner subsequently verifies it, and whether an allowed hint has been consumed. A PWP-inspired control baseline should use the same case and generator. The difference of interest is whether role control and assessment share delivered events and pharmacy-specific applicability conditions.

Where to cite it

Related work and the novelty discussion. Explicitly acknowledge the prior disclosure mechanism.

Possible manuscript wording

PWP controls patient information visibility through a disclosure grid. DualGraph-OSCE studies how disclosure and prompt events jointly constrain patient behavior and the interpretation of learner evidence. [R05·1]
