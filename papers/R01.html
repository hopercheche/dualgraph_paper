R01 · Education and pharmacy foundations

Consistent role portrayal and accurate performance records are separate requirements

The Association of Standardized Patient Educators (ASPE) Standards of Best Practice (SOBP)

Karen L. Lewis et al. · 2017
Advances in Simulation; peer-reviewed practice standards

Publisher / author sourceOriginal local PDF (8 pages)Cited passages and highlightsBack to references

What the study investigates

Standardized patients (SPs) portray a case and may also give feedback or record learner performance. These related responsibilities require different training. The article addresses how simulation activities can remain comparable across learners and encounters.

How it was studied

ASPE developed its standards through international expert consensus. They cover five domains: safe work environments, case development, SP training, program management, and professional development. Domain 3 treats role portrayal, feedback, and completion of assessment instruments separately. Trainers should check both the accuracy and consistency of portrayal and the accurate recording of observed learner behavior.

Findings

The output is a professional practice standard, rather than an experiment comparing AI with human actors. Sections 3.2 and 3.4 set out requirements for role training and assessment records, providing educational grounds for separate role-control and assessment endpoints.

Limits of the evidence

The standard does not define an 85% AI consistency pass mark or show that AI is more reliable than human SPs. Human training standards establish relevant requirements; they do not substitute for validation of an AI system.

How it informs DualGraph-OSCE

DualGraph-OSCE uses the same dialogue record for role constraints and assessment evidence, while keeping the two decisions distinct. A patient may faithfully repeat an incorrect regimen just stated by the student, making the role behavior compliant even though the student's advice is incorrect. The experiment therefore checks role events and learner requirements separately rather than merging them into a single unspecified accuracy score.

Where to cite it

Introduction: why role control and assessment both matter. Evaluation: the educational basis for two separate endpoints.

Possible manuscript wording

SP training requires consistent role portrayal and accurate performance records. These requirements motivate separate validation of virtual-role control and automated assessment. [R01·1]
