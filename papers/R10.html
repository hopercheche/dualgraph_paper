TemporalGuard already monitors multiround dialogue with temporal logic

Temporal Guardrails for LLM Conversations: A Runtime Verification Framework

Itay Cohen, Klaus Havelund, Moran Omer, Doron Peled · 2026
SAIV 2026; public author manuscript

Publisher / author sourceOriginal local PDF (22 pages)Cited passages and highlightsBack to references

What the study investigates

Single-turn safety checks cannot express rules such as permitting an action only after another action has occurred. The authors connect natural-language conversations to a formal runtime monitor.

How it was studied

Policies are expressed in past-time linear temporal logic. Speaker-tagged messages become propositional traces, and the monitor updates its state incrementally. Semantic grounding is compared using embeddings, NLI, hybrid approaches, and LLMs.

Findings

The authors report that LLM grounding generally outperforms their lightweight alternatives and demonstrate multiround safety cases. Deterministic guarantees apply to the propositions already extracted; grounding errors can still enter the monitor.

Limits of the evidence

State machines, past-time logic, incremental updates, and online blocking already have precedents. Adding temporal conditions alone is not the main innovation of DualGraph-OSCE. Deterministic monitoring does not make natural-language interpretation infallible.

How it informs DualGraph-OSCE

The first-order extension in R35 also handles cross-turn object binding. Our contribution must therefore focus on jointly executing conditions across role actions and assessment consequences. The EGJ engine retains unknown states and source evidence. In the migraine station, an allergy question may need a within-utterance span to establish that it preceded the first actual recommendation; the approved rule must not be silently strengthened into a requirement to receive an answer first.

Where to cite it

Related work: temporal monitoring. Method and discussion: the conditions under which deterministic guarantees hold.

Possible manuscript wording

Runtime-verification methods already map natural-language propositions to temporal monitors. DualGraph-OSCE focuses on the eligibility of pharmacy evidence and its consistent interpretation across role control and assessment. [R10·1]
