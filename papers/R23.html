R23 · Education and pharmacy foundations

CMM supplies clinical relation dimensions rather than an examination rubric

The Patient Care Process for Delivering Comprehensive Medication Management (CMM): Optimizing Medication Use in Patient-Centered, Team-Based Care Settings

CMM in Primary Care Research Team · 2018
ACCP public professional practice document; not an algorithm experiment

Publisher / author sourceOriginal local PDF (16 pages)Cited passages and highlightsBack to references

What the study investigates

Medication management requires more than knowing a drug's indication. Effectiveness, safety, and feasibility must be considered for the particular patient. The document describes a shared comprehensive medication management process.

How it was studied

Interviews and assessments across primary-care practices inform definitions of information gathering, assessment, care planning and implementation, and follow-up. Medication assessment addresses four dimensions: indication, effectiveness, safety, and adherence.

Findings

The output is a professional care process and its functions. Indications, interactions, doses, patient goals, and actual medication use need to be considered together, rather than memorized independently.

Limits of the evidence

It is not the CUHK four-station rubric or an experiment demonstrating a graph model's effectiveness. Its process order does not imply that every OSCE question must be asked in one fixed sequence.

How it informs DualGraph-OSCE

Clinical graph relations can follow these dimensions: drug–indication, patient–restriction, regimen–therapeutic objective, combined medication–risk, and behavior–use feasibility. CUHK's approved station rules select the applicable subgraph. A counselling station with a verified prescription must not turn every risk in the graph into a new mandatory examination item.

Where to cite it

Method: clinical knowledge organization and the distinction between professional knowledge and grading rules.

Possible manuscript wording

Clinical knowledge is organized around CMM's indication, effectiveness, safety, and adherence dimensions, while station materials define the assessment scope and required behaviors. [R23·1]
