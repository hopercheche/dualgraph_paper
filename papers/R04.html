R04 · Virtual patients and OSCEs

AIPatient already combines a knowledge graph with multiple patient agents

Simulated patient systems powered by large language model-based AI agents offer potential for transforming medical education

Huizi Yu et al. · 2026
Communications Medicine 6:27; published online in 2025

Publisher / author sourceOriginal local PDF (12 pages)Cited passages and highlightsBack to references

What the study investigates

The central problem is how clinical-record information can be turned into faithful, understandable patient responses that also convey different personalities.

How it was studied

AIPatient constructs a knowledge graph from clinical records. Retrieval, querying, abstraction, generation, rewriting, and checking agents implement a Reasoning RAG workflow. The authors separately evaluated knowledge extraction, response correctness, paraphrase robustness, and preservation of information under personality conditions. Twenty medical students participated in a crossover experience study. The source gives eight human SPs in the methods on PDF page 5 but ten in the results on page 7; this inconsistency should not be adopted as a sample-size precedent.

Findings

The full configuration reports 94.15% clinical-record question-answering accuracy and a knowledge-extraction F1 of 0.89. Performance differs by dataset, model, and configuration. These figures refer to QA and entity extraction, not OSCE grading accuracy.

Limits of the evidence

Multiagent generation, knowledge graphs, and checking are already combined here. That combination alone cannot be claimed as DualGraph-OSCE's contribution. Attribution of learner evidence, prompt consequences, and execution of faculty rules still require a different evaluation.

How it informs DualGraph-OSCE

The platform can draw on its division of responsibilities, but both comparison systems must receive the same case knowledge. Otherwise, a gain from additional knowledge cannot be distinguished from a gain in rule execution. The role generator receives an authorized view; private answer material is reserved for validation. This is an access policy, rather than unrestricted access to the whole graph for every agent.

Where to cite it

Related work: a close architectural predecessor. Evaluation: justification for equal knowledge conditions and a strong baseline.

Possible manuscript wording

AIPatient combines clinical-record knowledge graphs with multiagent retrieval, generation, and checking. DualGraph-OSCE examines the distinct eligibility of the same dialogue evidence for role control and learner assessment. [R04·1]
