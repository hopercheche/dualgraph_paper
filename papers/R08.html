R08 · Rule and evidence algorithms

Rulers already requires scores to carry verifiable textual evidence

From Rubrics to Reliable Scores: Evidence-Grounded Text Evaluation with LLM Judges

Yihan Hong, Huaiyuan Yao, Bolin Shen, Wanpeng Xu, Hua Wei, Yushun Dong · 2026
arXiv:2601.08654; Rulers framework; preprint

Publisher / author sourceOriginal local PDF (21 pages)Cited passages and highlightsBack to references

What the study investigates

When a human rubric is used by an LLM judge, criterion interpretation may drift, score explanations may lack support, and model scores may differ from the instructor's scale.

How it was studied

Rulers combines a locked scoring specification, evidence-grounded execution, and a post-processing mapping learned on an independent calibration set. Evidence must be located in the assessed text and checked according to its type. Verification includes more than literal span matching: it also checks different kinds of claims and handles low confidence or review. The experiments concern essay and text-generation evaluation, rather than pharmacy OSCEs.

Findings

The paper reports scoring agreement and evidence-auditability results across datasets, with analyses of specification locking, evidence, and calibration. Because calibration uses human labels, the gains cannot all be attributed to prompting or graph structure.

Limits of the evidence

Attaching quotations to scores is not new. A real quotation can still be a patient's answer, a negated recommendation, an outdated dose, or a frequency belonging to a different drug. Literal verification alone does not establish that it is valid learner evidence.

How it informs DualGraph-OSCE

Do not portray Rulers as checking quotation existence only: it already uses typed evidence verification. DualGraph-OSCE concerns the joint execution of OSCE-specific evidence conditions across role authorization and assessment consequences. Its baseline should retain span checks and the history of delivered prompts. A comparison must not deprive the baseline of evidence to manufacture a benefit.

Where to cite it

Related work, evidence-eligibility checks, and the distinction from evidence-grounded scoring.

Possible manuscript wording

Rulers improves scoring auditability through locked criteria and verifiable evidence. Pharmacy dialogue additionally requires determining who produced the evidence, which regimen it describes, and whether it represents an actual action. [R08·1]
