RubricsTree is a strong predecessor for structured clinical criteria

RubricsTree: Scalable and Evolving Open-Ended Evaluation of Personal Health Agents across Health Memory and Medical Skills

Weizhi Zhang et al. · 2026
arXiv:2606.18203; preprint

Publisher / author sourceOriginal local PDF (32 pages)Cited passages and highlightsBack to references

What the study investigates

Open-ended personal-health responses are costly to evaluate one at a time with experts, while generic LLM judges can miss detailed clinical requirements.

How it was studied

The method organizes medical evaluation into a hierarchy with verifiable Boolean leaves. Expert iteration and criterion routing select the requirements applicable to a question. The public manuscript describes more than 100 atomic criteria and more than 4,000 real user questions, with expert annotation and context perturbations.

Findings

Results support the use of structured clinical criteria and expert involvement for scalable evaluation and locating specific weaknesses. The object is personal-health answers and memory, rather than learner actions in four pharmacy OSCE stations.

Limits of the evidence

Hierarchical rubrics, applicability routing, and atomic clinical checks already exist. ICC choices and continuous-score assumptions cannot be transferred without explanation to OSCE outputs that contain only ordinal or categorical labels.

How it informs DualGraph-OSCE

The Rule Graph should include applicability conditions, rather than turn the entire clinical knowledge base into a marking scheme. Interaction knowledge may be available in the diuretics and MTX stations, but only approved counselling requirements are graded. Clinical correctness and examination scope remain distinct, with an explicit reason for applicability or non-applicability.

Where to cite it

Related work: structured clinical evaluation. Method: applicability routing. Evaluation: unjustified penalties for out-of-scope criteria.

Possible manuscript wording

Clinical evaluation already uses hierarchical routing of atomic criteria. DualGraph-OSCE additionally executes applicability conditions determined by station scope, dialogue attribution, and delivered role actions. [R16·1]
