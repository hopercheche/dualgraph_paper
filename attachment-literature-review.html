Corrections to the supplied related-work text

  • Brügge’s article number is 1391, not 1349; the first author is Emilia Brügge.
  • Duan was published on 7 January 2025 in the journal’s 2024 volume. Its SUS acronym denotes Safety, Usability, Smoothness, rather than the standard System Usability Scale.
  • Zhang evaluated a human-SP consultation with automated listening and scoring. It did not establish the accuracy of an autonomous conversational patient role.
  • Trevena’s graph describes physiological state transitions. It does not demonstrate LLM conversational role consistency or OSCE grade accuracy.
  • Wu reviews undergraduate medical virtual simulation; it is not a pharmacy-specific review or proof of improved clinical transfer.
  • Song’s case-derived data are retrospective. Its clinical GraphRAG results support a strong knowledge-grounded comparator but not deterministic OSCE safety.
  • Citation counts in the attachment are not carried into the manuscript. They are time-dependent and do not determine whether a claim is supported.

The argument these sources can support

LLM simulations are educationally plausible (Brügge), automated assessment should be checked against explicit human-reference items (Zhang), and knowledge-grounded generation is a credible baseline (Song and, more narrowly, Duan). Explicit graph rule execution has prior clinical precedents (Trevena). These findings motivate the specific test in DualGraph-OSCE: can delivered-event evidence jointly support separate role permissions and assessment consequences more reliably than a grounded role LLM followed by grounded post-hoc assessment?

None of these sources proves that separating role generation and assessment necessarily causes the observed problems. That is a hypothesis to test with a fair, paired comparison. Likewise, no cited paper supplies results for the present 16-recording cohort or 120-turn benchmark. Claims of eliminated leakage or exact concordance require the actual faculty labels, system outputs, denominators, and uncertainty estimates.
