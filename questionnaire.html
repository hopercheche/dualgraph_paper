Consultation experience questionnaire

Five short items and two optional open questions.

Complete this questionnaire after the assigned consultations and after opening any available feedback. It takes about three minutes. Answers do not affect course grades. Open questions may be left blank.

This page does not upload or automatically save responses. Download the CSV and return it as instructed by the research assistant. Do not enter identifying information.

If feedback is unavailable, select “Unable to judge” for Q4 and Q5.

These are study-specific experience items, not a validated composite scale. Report responses separately; satisfaction does not establish role compliance or assessment accuracy. [R33·1]
