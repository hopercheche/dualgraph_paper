How long is a pharmacy OSCE context?

A source-material token audit, with a clear distinction between documents, dialogue and actual requests.

Complex evidence is supported; window overflow is not

ClaimEvidence in the source snapshotUse in the paper
Pharmacy decisions depend on several conditionsThe stations cover mechanism, dose, missed doses, adverse effects, patient history and follow-up; conditions are distributed across dialogue.Bind each event to its speaker, medicine, patient, regimen and time.
All reference material is too large to send indiscriminatelyStation-specific professional PDF packages contain approximately 82,817–271,057 reference tokens before relevance selection.Motivate retrieval. A complete consultation necessarily exceeds the model windowNot established. Recognised text contains about 0.6K–2.4K tokens; full manual transcripts and the runtime model window were not verified in this audit.Do not write that real consultations already overflow the context window.
Models may struggle to use information within a large windowGeneral controlled studies show position, association, update and interference effects; these vary by model and task.Use as motivation, not as measured pharmacy OSCE performance.
Graph-selected evidence must be more accurateNot established. Extraction can omit events, misattribute speech or lose corrections.Test against a strong shared-input baseline and retain source spans and uncertainty.

Count rubrics, clinical references and application fields separately

Sending every PDF in a station package is neither a realistic nor a fair baseline. Both A and B should use the same retrieval source snapshot and candidate pool, with role-appropriate access. A professionally curated knowledge graph and a Rule Graph serve different purposes: the former stores clinical relations and provenance; the latter executes the approved station permissions and assessment constraints.

Per-document token extraction and hashes

What long-context papers support

PaperExperiment and findingRelevance and limit
Lost in the Middle · TACL 2024 [R36·1]Multi-document QA varied answer position. Some models performed worse when relevant information appeared in the middle.Test evidence-position sensitivity; older general QA results are not pharmacy OSCE measurements.
RULER · COLM 2024 [R37·2]Thirteen task types, 17 models, 4K–128K inputs; includes multi-hop and aggregation. Some models declined substantially, while Gemini 1.5 Pro remained comparatively strong.Nominal context capacity differs from effective use. Do not claim every model fails as context grows.
NoLiMa · ICML 2025 [R38·2]Weakens lexical overlap between question and evidence. At 32K, 11 of 13 models fell below half their short-context baseline in the reported tasks.Motivates semantic association tests; its numerical decline cannot be copied as our result.
LongMemEval · ICLR 2025 [R39·2]Five hundred cross-session questions cover updates, time and abstention. Fact or summary compression can discard information.Keep original spans, corrections, counterevidence and missingness; shorter is not automatically more accurate.

How DualGraph-OSCE uses this evidence

  1. Extract each newly delivered act with its source span, speaker, medication bindings and relation to earlier events.
  2. Retrieve the events needed by the currently applicable rules, including counterevidence and corrections.
  3. Use semantic matching to propose alignments; deterministically execute confirmed rules to produce separate role permissions and assessment states.
  4. If decisive evidence is missing or uncertain, expand the source context or flag review rather than invent a conclusion.

The framework is not a generic dialogue summary. Its claim concerns explicit relationship execution and common event provenance. Context reduction is a process measure; the primary outcomes remain role compliance and assessment exact match.

A claim the paper can support now

Pharmacy OSCE assessment requires information from multiple sources and time points to be bound to the correct patient, medicine and student action. The supplied materials show that reference documents and assessment instructions can be substantially larger than the dialogue itself. We therefore evaluate whether paired Rule–Evidence Graphs improve the explicit use of attributable, temporally ordered evidence, rather than assuming that ordinary consultations exceed the model context window.

Results about superior compliance, eliminated leakage or exact concordance require the actual frozen benchmark and faculty-verified outputs. They must not be inferred from these token counts.
