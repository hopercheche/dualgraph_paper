RESEARCH AND STUDY MATERIALS

Sample-size rationale and dependence

A balanced technical benchmark, with recording-level uncertainty.

What 120 means

Thirty checkpoints from each of four stations gives 120 A/B comparisons. Each comparison asks both methods to handle the same frozen clinical situation, then checks their outputs against the same faculty reference. It is a benchmark target, not a universal minimum sample size.

For example, if A and B both get 90 records right, their totals alone hide the crucial question: did B fix A’s errors, or did the two methods fail on different records? Paired analysis keeps the two outputs together.

Why 16 recordings do not become 120 independent observations

Several checkpoints taken from one consultation share its participant, case facts, wording and earlier dialogue. Think of them as several questions drawn from the same story: they provide useful technical coverage but not as much independent information as 120 unrelated consultations.

Retain recording-level provenance, give station-specific descriptive counts and use source-cluster uncertainty. If the same participant made several recordings, preserve participant linkage for sensitivity analysis. With few source clusters, intervals may remain wide.

The earlier planning illustration

An independence-based planning example assumed that B alone would succeed on 20% of pairs and A alone on 5%, giving a 15-point net advantage. With a two-sided per-endpoint threshold of 0.025, an exact paired-binomial/McNemar calculation estimated about 80% power near 108 independent pairs and about 85% at 120. These are assumptions, not collected results.

That calculation cannot be applied unchanged when 120 checkpoints come from approximately 16 recordings. It also does not establish 80% power for each station, guarantee significant results or determine how many students should be recruited. Recompute precision after the final cluster structure and pilot effect estimates are known.

Lakens, Sample Size Justification supports stating the actual rationale and precision limits. Fagerland et al., McNemar methods explains paired categorical testing.

How to state the scope

The primary claim concerns rule enforcement and role behaviour on fixed pharmacy OSCE stations. It is not a powered estimate of learning gain, clinical patient safety, generalisation to all pharmacy cases or superiority to human standardised patients. Natural student use supports feasibility and identifies failures; a different study is needed for those broader claims.
